1. Project overview

Pilot conducted on the River Nene by Corby & District Sub Aqua Club (CADSAC) with support from project partners and the British Sub-Aqua Jubilee Trust.

2. Why freshwater invertebrates?

Freshwater macroinvertebrates are internationally recognised bioindicators of river health.

  • Stonefly and mayfly larvae indicate clean, well-oxygenated water
  • Chironomid midges and water louse tolerate poor water quality
  • Diversity of groups matters as much as individual species presence
  • Repeated monitoring at fixed sites reveals trends over time
